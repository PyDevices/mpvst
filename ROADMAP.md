# Roadmap

MPVST is heading toward a soundtrack and an effects library that move with the
rebuilt audioeffects.

## Next

- The soundtrack pieces that use effects (AureliaOverture, VelvetCircuit,
  Perihelion, ShimmerLab) move to the rebuilt audioeffects, re-voiced where
  the new effects call for it. `tools/test-effects-lib.py` moves with them.

Bugs, and things you need that don't work yet, go to
[issues](https://github.com/PyDevices/mpvst/issues).
